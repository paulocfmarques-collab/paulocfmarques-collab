## 🎯 Executive Summary

**Senior Embedded Systems Engineer** with **15+ years** of expertise in designing and leading high-reliability software solutions across:

- **Embedded Systems & Firmware Architecture** — ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, real-time RTOS
- **IoT & Networked Systems** — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, MQTT, UDP/TCP, distributed device control
- **Observability & Automation** — Prometheus, Grafana, PostgreSQL, network discovery, DevOps
- **Technical Leadership** — Team management, architecture design, mentorship, agile engineering

Currently serving as **Team Leader at AEL Sistemas** (high-reliability avionics software) and **University Professor** (embedded systems & technology development).

> **Philosophy:** Build systems that are **reliable**, **observable**, and **maintainable**; lead teams with **technical rigor**, **continuous learning**, and **engineering excellence**.
